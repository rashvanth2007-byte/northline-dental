# Topic Plan: first pages for a Tamil Nadu dental clinic site

**Template:** Northline (sample clinic: Northline Dental Co., R.S. Puram, Coimbatore, open 24/7)
**Audience:** families in the clinic's town searching on their phones, often in English with Tamil words ("pal vali", "teeth cleaning near me"), and parents booking for children or elderly parents
**Date:** 22 October 2026
**Method:** topic planning from the clinic's own treatments and the questions already on the page. Difficulty is an estimate (no keyword-volume tool was used); check volumes in Google Search Console or Keyword Planner once the site is live.

Replace "Coimbatore" and "R.S. Puram" with each client's own town and area. Pages about one town only rank in that town, which is exactly the point.

## Ground rules for every page (Indian dental advertising ethics)

- No testimonials, star ratings, review counts or before/after photos. Link to the clinic's Google profile instead.
- No "best", "No. 1", "painless", "guaranteed" or "lowest price". Say what is done and what it costs.
- Fees are "from ₹…" starting fees, with what is not included ("a crown is charged separately").
- Each page names the reviewing dentist with degree, Tamil Nadu Dental Council registration number and date, like the site's "Information reviewed by…" lines.
- Serious symptoms (facial swelling, fever, trouble swallowing or breathing) always point to a hospital emergency department.
- Write like the clinic talks: short sentences, "we", plain Indian English, a Tamil heading or phrase where it helps (பல் வலி, முதல் வருகை).

## Topic Details

### 1. Root Canal Treatment in Coimbatore: Cost and Steps
- **Keyword**: root canal treatment cost coimbatore (also: RCT cost, root canal near me, வேர் சிகிச்சை)
- **Intent**: commercial
- **Funnel**: MOFU
- **Type**: service guide
- **Difficulty**: medium
- **Why**: RCT is the most searched treatment after tooth pain, and the first worry is cost and whether it hurts.
- **Angle**: the clinic's own written-estimate habit applied to the page. A clear "from ₹4,500" fee, what it covers, what costs extra (the crown), and how many sittings, with no scare words or "painless" claims.
- **Key sections**: signs you may need an RCT; what happens in each sitting; fees and what changes them; does it hurt (honest answer, anaesthesia, going at your pace); aftercare and when to call.

### 2. Tooth Pain at Night? What to Do Before You Reach Us
- **Keyword**: tooth pain at night what to do (also: pal vali, toothache relief at home)
- **Intent**: informational
- **Funnel**: TOFU
- **Type**: how-to
- **Difficulty**: low
- **Why**: people search this at midnight, which is exactly when a 24/7 clinic can help. It is the strongest bridge to the emergency strip and the WhatsApp button.
- **Angle**: safe, plain first steps (rinse, cold compress, a pain reliever the patient already takes safely), what not to do (aspirin on the gum, heat), and clear hospital red flags. It ends with "call or WhatsApp us now".
- **Key sections**: quick safe steps; what not to do; red flags that need a hospital; when to see a dentist; how to reach the clinic tonight.

### 3. Dental Implant Cost in Coimbatore, Explained Simply
- **Keyword**: dental implant cost in coimbatore (also: single tooth implant price)
- **Intent**: commercial
- **Funnel**: MOFU
- **Type**: service guide
- **Difficulty**: medium
- **Why**: implants are the highest-value treatment, and patients compare prices across clinics before they visit.
- **Angle**: break the fee into parts (implant, abutment, crown, scans, bone graft if needed), say why quotes differ between clinics, and offer EMI on request, without naming finance companies the clinic hasn't confirmed.
- **Key sections**: who implants suit; the steps and timeline; what the "from ₹25,000" covers; why quotes differ; caring for an implant; FAQs.

### 4. Your Child's First Dental Visit: A Parent's Guide
- **Keyword**: child first dental visit age (also: kids dentist coimbatore)
- **Intent**: informational
- **Funnel**: TOFU
- **Type**: beginner's guide
- **Difficulty**: low
- **Why**: parents book for children, and calm, practical advice builds trust before the first booking.
- **Angle**: a warm walk through the visit from the child's side, with a Tamil line or two parents can use at home, and the "Appointment For: My Child" option in the booking form.
- **Key sections**: when to go (around the first birthday); how to prepare your child; what happens in the chair; habits at home (brushing, sweets, night bottles); booking for your child.

### 5. Clear Aligners vs Braces: Which Suits You?
- **Keyword**: clear aligners vs braces india
- **Intent**: commercial
- **Funnel**: MOFU
- **Type**: comparison
- **Difficulty**: medium
- **Why**: teens, young adults and parents weigh looks, cost and time, and need a fair comparison, not a sales page.
- **Angle**: an honest side-by-side table (cost from ₹…, time, visits, food rules, who each suits) with "your orthodontist decides after an examination". Only say "orthodontist" if the clinic has one with an MDS.
- **Key sections**: how each works; side-by-side table; who should choose which; costs and EMI; what the first consultation involves.

### 6. Bleeding Gums: Causes and When to See a Dentist
- **Keyword**: bleeding gums causes
- **Intent**: informational
- **Funnel**: TOFU
- **Type**: explainer
- **Difficulty**: low
- **Why**: a very common worry that leads directly to cleaning and gum treatment, the clinic's most frequent visit.
- **Angle**: plain causes (plaque, brushing too hard, some medicines, diabetes and pregnancy), what scaling actually involves, and the cleaning fee.
- **Key sections**: common causes; when it is urgent; what scaling and polishing involve; home care; booking a cleaning.

### 7. Dental Treatment Fees in Coimbatore: A Simple Guide
- **Keyword**: dental treatment cost coimbatore
- **Intent**: commercial
- **Funnel**: MOFU
- **Type**: guide (hub for fees)
- **Difficulty**: medium
- **Why**: price is the top question, and the site already publishes starting fees, so a page explaining them earns links and searches.
- **Angle**: the fee table from the site with a plain line on what each includes, why exact fees come only after a consultation, payment options (UPI, cards, cash, EMI) and insurance receipts.
- **Key sections**: fee table; what changes a fee; the written estimate; payment and EMI; insurance claims.

### 8. 24-Hour Emergency Dentist in R.S. Puram, Coimbatore
- **Keyword**: emergency dentist coimbatore (also: 24 hours dental clinic near me)
- **Intent**: transactional
- **Funnel**: BOFU
- **Type**: location page
- **Difficulty**: medium
- **Why**: people with pain search "near me" and call the first clear result. This page targets that search directly.
- **Angle**: only for clinics that really have a dentist available round the clock. It covers what counts as a dental emergency, how to reach the clinic (map, landmark, parking), and "we'll try to see you as soon as possible" wording, never a promised time.
- **Key sections**: what counts as an emergency; call or WhatsApp now; how to find us (map, landmark, parking); hospital red flags; what to bring.

## Per-page checklist

- Title under 60 characters with the town name; meta description about 150 characters with a call to action.
- One H1, the Tamil subtitle under the main H2s, an "Information reviewed by…" line.
- A `MedicalWebPage` or `Article` JSON-LD block with `reviewedBy` (the dentist) and `lastReviewed`; `FAQPage` only for questions that are visible on the page.
- The clinic's own photos with real alt text, compressed; no patient faces, no before/after.
- Book, Call and WhatsApp buttons, and a link to "How to Find Us".
