    Care You Can Count On ஏன் எங்களைத் தேர்வு செய்ய வேண்டும்?

    How you feel during a visit matters as much as the result. Here are four things we do at every appointment.

    • Your Comfort First

      Numbing gel before any injection, computer-controlled local anaesthesia, and conscious sedation optionsgentle local anaesthesia, and extra time for anxious patients.

    • Fees in Writing

      Before we begin, you get a written plan with every option and its cost. No hidden charges, and no pressure to decide the same day.

    • Strict Sterilisation

      Instruments sterilised in a Class B autoclave after every patient, single-use items, and sealed pouches opened in front of you.

    • The Right Specialist for Each Treatment

      Root canals by an endodontist, braces and aligners by an orthodontist, implants by a prosthodontist, and children by a paediatric dentist.

    • The Same Dentist Every Visit

      Your dentist knows your history and your worries, from the first visit to every follow-up.
